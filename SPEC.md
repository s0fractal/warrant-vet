## [ADR-0001. The subject is a descriptor of exact bytes](docs/adr/0001-the-subject-is-a-descriptor-of-exact-bytes.md)

- **WVET-D1** ([contract](contracts/WVET-D1.json)). A review's subject MUST be the SHA-256 of the canonical descriptor `{"profile","ecosystem","name","version","digest"}` whose members are ASCII strings; a review of any other descriptor, including the same name and version with another digest, MUST NOT count for an artifact.
- **WVET-D2** ([contract](contracts/WVET-D2.json)). A descriptor MUST be built from the lockfile's own digest: a registry entry without a digest and a git or path dependency MUST be reported `UNPINNED`, a workspace member `LOCAL`, and a name or version that is not ASCII MUST refuse the lockfile.

## [ADR-0002. Trust and binding come from the reader](docs/adr/0002-trust-and-binding-come-from-the-reader.md)

- **WVET-T1** ([contract](contracts/WVET-T1.json)). A check MUST require a trust config supplied by the reader; reviewers are bound to keys only through it.
- **WVET-T2** ([contract](contracts/WVET-T2.json)). The reader's warrant MUST verify the review store at settlement grade with the required-binding option; a record it reports with an `ERR` MUST NOT count, a store-level `ERR` or a missing report MUST refuse the run, and warnings MUST NOT change a verdict.

## [ADR-0003. Policy and verdicts](docs/adr/0003-policy-and-verdicts.md)

- **WVET-P1** ([contract](contracts/WVET-P1.json)). Only `accept` and `reject` records whose subject is the artifact's descriptor and whose `under` contains the policy's criteria blob MUST count.
- **WVET-P2** ([contract](contracts/WVET-P2.json)). An active `reject` by a counted reviewer MUST make the artifact `REJECTED`, whatever the accepts.
- **WVET-P3** ([contract](contracts/WVET-P3.json)). An artifact MUST be `ADMITTED` only when at least `min_accepts` distinct counted reviewers have an active `accept`; when `reviewers` is given, only those reviewers count.
- **WVET-P4** ([contract](contracts/WVET-P4.json)). A review MUST stop counting when its own actor files a `supersede` naming it, and only then.
- **WVET-P5** ([contract](contracts/WVET-P5.json)). `check` MUST exit 0 only when every lockfile entry is `ADMITTED` or `LOCAL`, and MUST refuse a policy with unknown members, an empty criteria, or `min_accepts` below 1.
